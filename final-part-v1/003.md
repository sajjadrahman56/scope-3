# 2. Why do BEACON rules examine all 25 features?

Because **data quality is not the same as predictive usefulness**.

Imagine:

| Feature                   |  Predictive usefulness |              Data-quality importance |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------: | -----------------------------------: |
| Product Weight            |                   High |                                 High |
| PCF Protocol              |            High/Medium |                                 High |
| Company                   |                   High |                                 High |
| Product Detail            |                 Medium |                                 High |
| PCF-ID                    |      None as predictor | **High for uniqueness/traceability** |
| Source for Product Weight |          None/directly |            **High for traceability** |
| Relative Change in PCF    | Possibly inappropriate |             **High for consistency** |
| Company-reported reason   |                    Low |        **High for interpretability** |
| Upstream CO₂e             |     Potentially useful |                                 High |
| End-of-Life CO₂e          |     Potentially useful |                                 High |

For example, **PCF-ID** may be useless for predicting PCF.

But BEACON absolutely should examine whether:

```text
PCF-ID
```

is unique.

That's exactly what R08 does.

So you can say:

> **A feature may be excluded from the predictive model while remaining relevant to data-quality assurance.**

That is actually an important methodological distinction in your dissertation.

# 9. And here's an important point about your 84.98% Representativeness

Don't simply modify the data until you get:

> 100%

That would be bad research.

Your R15 score of **69.97%** may indicate that the Carbon Catalogue is structurally unbalanced.

For example:

```text
Some industries → many products
Some industries → very few products

Some regions → many records
Some regions → very few records
```

That's not necessarily a data-entry error.

It's potentially a **dataset limitation**.

And this can actually become part of your RQ1 findings:

> "BEACON identified representativeness as the principal quality limitation, reflecting uneven distribution across categories/regions/industries."

Then we decide whether the issue should be:

* corrected,
* documented,
* handled through modelling,
* or retained as a limitation.

# 13. One thing we need to check carefully

Your **Company target encoding** needs to be performed correctly.

Because you're doing:

```text
Company → Target Encoding
```

and your target is:

```text
PCF
```

we **must fit the target encoder only on the training data**, never on the full dataset before the train/test split.

Otherwise the test set's PCF information leaks into the Company feature.

Correct:

```text
866
 │
 ├── Train
 │     ↓
 │   Fit Company target encoder
 │     ↓
 │   Transform Train
 │
 └── Test
       ↓
   Transform using TRAIN encoder
```

Not:

```text
866
 ↓
Target encode Company using all PCF
 ↓
Train/Test split
```

That would contaminate the evaluation.

This is especially important for your dissertation because you're explicitly studying **data quality and model reliability**.
